# NaviPlay — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 May 2026

NaviPlay is a music player application for Android tablets and Android TV, designed to connect to your self-hosted music server.

NaviPlay does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

---

## Data Collection

NaviPlay does not collect any personal information, analytics, or usage data.

The app does not:

- Create user accounts
- Track users
- Use advertising
- Use analytics services
- Transmit personal data to the developer or third parties

---

## Server Connections

NaviPlay connects directly to your self-hosted music server using the Subsonic REST API.

Compatible servers include Navidrome and other Subsonic-compatible servers.

Any connection details (server URL, username, or authentication token) are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted to the developer or any third party.

---

## Permissions

NaviPlay requests the following permissions, used only for core playback functionality:

- **Internet access** (`INTERNET`) — to connect to your self-hosted music server
- **Notifications** (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`) — to display playback controls in the notification shade and on the lock screen
- **Foreground service** (`FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`) — to continue audio playback in the background while the app is not in the foreground

No permission is used for tracking, data collection, or advertising.

---

## Third-Party Services

NaviPlay does not use:

- Analytics tools
- Advertising SDKs
- Tracking technologies

The app communicates only with the user-configured music server.

Third-party open-source libraries used within the app are listed in the NOTICES file included with the application. These libraries operate entirely on-device and do not independently transmit data.

---

## Data Security

All data remains on the user's device or within their self-hosted server. NaviPlay does not operate any cloud infrastructure.

Users are responsible for securing their own server and network configuration.

---

## Children's Privacy

NaviPlay does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

---

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be reflected in the version distributed via the Google Play Store. The "last updated" date at the top of this document indicates when the policy was last revised.
